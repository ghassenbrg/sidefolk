# Studio Collection — Sidefolk at work

Six additional characters for an office, client meeting, shared workspace or a quieter personal desktop. The collection is restrained and friendly, not dressed-up cartoons. See [the paired 2D/3D board](images/08-professional-crew.png).

## Characters

| Character | Visual signature | Suggested pairing | Motion personality |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Calder** | Slate owl, angular brow, cream belly, thin mint collar | Research, analysis, planning | Small head turn; steady gaze |
| **Finch** | Sand bird, charcoal folded wing, mint wing tab | Writing, communications, notes | Brief nod; occasional wing adjustment |
| **Orbit** | Ivory robot, dark faceplate, mint horizontal eyes | Integrations, operations, tooling | Precise head pivot; restrained wave |
| **Slate** | Charcoal penguin, ivory belly, mint lapel-shaped patch | Main work companion, project coordination | Grounded stance; one small nod |
| **Vale** | Compact terracotta fox, broad curled tail, thin mint neck band | Product, design, delivery | Slight lean; slow tail settle |
| **Dot** | Ivory capsule, two tiny dark eyes, mint side stripe | Minimal desktop presence, any project | Gentle tilt; static while reading |

**Recommended work default: Slate.** Its simple vertical silhouette reads clearly at small sizes and has less visual detail than the original soft character family. **Dot** is the most neutral choice for people who want a status companion without an animal mascot. **Calder** suits a thoughtful, understated identity. These are optional preferences, not assumptions about someone's profession or ability.

Each character has 2D and 3D concepts on the board. The roster now has 18 character identities in total. Professional is a collection/presentation preset, not a renderer: users can choose a professional 2D or professional 3D pet. Pip, Byte and Patch can also receive quiet styling without changing identity.

## Work-friendly art rules

- Compact silhouettes, matte surfaces, small ordinary eyes, no sparkles, blush, toys or noisy costumes.
- One identifying accent. Keep mint subtle; preserve charcoal/cream contrast on light and dark wallpapers.
- Calm neutral poses. Finish with a short nod, not a jump or confetti burst. A failed task stays legible without exaggerated distress.
- At roughly 96–120 px, reduce surface details. Offer the regular size or larger text independently; workplace use does not imply tiny controls.
- Shared orb and request labels remain consistent. Suppressing decorative motion must not hide a waiting request.
- No roleplay language in work messages. “Website refresh needs your answer” works as well as “Slate has a question.”

## Proposed professional preset

Selecting this preset **shows proposed changes before applying them**: restrained appearance, reduced decorative motion, sound cues off, desktop presence only when needed, and preview text limited to project name + status. Let the user edit every choice. Never silently change agent connections or authorization policies with a cosmetic preset.

Offer **Menu bar only** for people who want no visible desktop character. Clicking the menu icon opens Home/Requests; a waiting badge stays available. This is a proposed mode, not a claim the current implementation offers the complete experience.

Keep **Hide from screen recordings** explicit. The app currently uses platform content protection; it should describe that supported capture tools may hide the overlay, not promise universal protection or automatic meeting detection. Hiding character previews and details is useful regardless of screen-capture support.

## Production considerations

None of these six named designs is a finished model or sprite set. Vale could begin from the existing fox rig, but its illustrated seated pose needs an upright animation study. Calder/Finch/Slate require wing-to-arm and prop-anchor studies. Dot has no visible arms: use a separate anchored alert sign and confirm the renderer contract, rather than stretching hidden geometry to imitate a paw. Orbit and Relay should remain distinct: Orbit is quiet and minimal, Relay is expressive and playful.

Keep the selected **Pip Signal** bunny as the brand mark across all collections. A bunny brand does not require everyone to choose a bunny companion.
