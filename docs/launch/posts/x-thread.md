# X (Twitter): launch thread

**When:** the same morning as the main launch, pinned to the founder's profile. Each post is under 280
characters. Media in brackets; a short screen recording beats a still for posts 1 and 3.

---

**1/** [video: the first 15 seconds of the demo, or `screenshots/hero-light.png`]

This is Sidefolk. A small companion that lives on your Mac desktop.

It keeps your notes, reminds you on time and watches pages and files while you work.

Out today for macOS. `[FOUNDER: link]`

**2/** [`screenshots/ask-panel-light.png`]

You tell it things in plain words.

"remind me tomorrow at 9 to send the deck"
"add oat milk to my groceries list"
"tell me when report.pdf appears in Downloads"

These run on your Mac in a moment. No AI, no account.

**3/** [video: a page watch firing, or `screenshots/live-values-light.png` with `bubble-reminder-light.png`]

It keeps watch so you don't have to: a pricing page that changed, a site that went down, localhost:3000
coming back up, the CPU every 10 seconds.

When something matters, it comes to you with the buttons you need.

**4/** [`screenshots/settings-ai-light.png`]

When you want answers, bring the AI you already use: Claude Code or Codex on your plan, an API key, or
Ollama and LM Studio on your Mac.

The AI can only act through Sidefolk's tools. A fixed set of rules decides what runs, not the model.

**5/** [`screenshots/bubble-command-failed-light.png`]

For people who live in a terminal:

sidefolk run -- npm test

It tells you when it's done, and when it fails, the lines that matter. Claude Code and Codex can leave you
notes and reminders over MCP.

**6/** [`screenshots/ask-apple-notes-light.png`]

It can copy your Apple Notes into its notebook, organised. It never removes a note from Notes unless you
ask, and it asks you each time.

**7/** [`Resources/Character/companions.png`]

Mochi is the default. There are twelve others, from the original robot to Brew and its cup of coffee.

It speaks ten languages and holds still when nothing is happening.

**8/**

It's a first version: Apple silicon, macOS 15+. Everything stays on your Mac.

I'd love to hear what you'd want it to watch. `[FOUNDER: link]`
