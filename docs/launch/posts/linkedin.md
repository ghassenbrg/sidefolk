Today I'm releasing Sidefolk 0.1.0, a small companion for the Mac desktop.

Most AI lives in a chat box: you open it, ask, read, close it, and it forgets. I wanted the opposite. A
small character that stays on my desktop, keeps what I tell it, and comes to me when something matters.

What it does today:

→ Notes, ideas and reminders in plain words, handled on the Mac with no AI at all
→ Watches web pages, sites, files and long-running commands, and tells you when they change
→ Works with the AI you already pay for (Claude Code, Codex) or your own models
→ Lets coding agents leave you notes and reminders over MCP
→ Keeps everything on your Mac: no account, no tracking
→ Speaks ten languages

The part I care most about is trust. The AI never decides what it's allowed to do; a fixed set of rules
does. It only works in folders you share, deleting means the Trash, and every action is listed with who
asked for it.

It's a first version, Apple silicon only, and there's plenty still to build. If you try it, I'd like to
hear what you'd want it to keep an eye on.
