## Maker's first comment

> Hi Product Hunt, I'm `[FOUNDER: name]`, and I made Sidefolk.
>
> I wanted something between a to-do app and a chat window: a small character that stays on my desktop,
> keeps what I tell it and comes to me when it matters. So that's what Sidefolk is.
>
> - Type "remind me tomorrow at 9 to send the deck" or "tell me when report.pdf appears in Downloads"
>   and it's done on your Mac, without AI.
> - When you want answers, it uses the AI you already have: Claude Code, Codex, an API key, or a model
>   on your own Mac.
> - Your coding agents can leave you notes and reminders, and `sidefolk run -- npm test` tells you when
>   a long command is done.
> - It copies your Apple Notes into its notebook, organised, and you can talk to it if you like.
> - Everything stays on your Mac. No account, no tracking.
>
> It's the first version: Apple silicon only, no automatic updates yet, and typing commands without AI
> works in English only. I'd love to know what you'd want it to keep an eye on.
>
