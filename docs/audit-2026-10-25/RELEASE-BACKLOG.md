## Proposed execution order

1. **Joint decision:** R01 after Claude's independent report. Freeze new features while deciding.
2. **Trust and correctness:** R02–R07 and R11 as applicable. These come before asking beta users to rely on their own data or grant authority.
3. **Complete understandable workflows:** R08–R13 and R22, then a small observed preflight. Fold unnecessary screens/options away according to the chosen scope; preserve existing data and approved art.
4. **Native and release proof:** R14–R17, R19–R20. R15 applies only to the developer capabilities offered. R18 is avoided by deferring those execution features or must pass if they stay exposed.
5. **Private beta and positioning decision:** R21. Fix failures, repeat affected tasks, extend the accumulating-note test. Graduating to public v1 requires the stable scope's full acceptance evidence, not completion of every deferred item.

## Features that do not earn priority by being implemented already

Further character styles/import conveniences, calendar/briefing expansion, embedded-terminal features, background automation, broad session teams, voice and screenshots remain parked unless the beta identifies them as the reason the chosen core job fails. The existing logo and approved character assets are retained. No deletion or rewrite is authorized by this document.
